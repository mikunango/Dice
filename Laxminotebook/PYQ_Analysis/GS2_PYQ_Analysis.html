Key patterns

  • Asked in all 10 years: Federalism / Centre–State (incl. GST, UTs, Finance Commission), Constitutional & statutory bodies, and governance (civil services / e-governance / Citizens’ Charter).
  • Asked every year since 2018: Comparative constitutions – India vs USA / UK / France (10 questions in 9 years).
  • Almost every year: Local government (8 of 10 years), Elections / RPA / ECI (8 of 10 years), Pressure groups & civil society (9 of 10 years).
  • Exact topic repeats: CAG (2018, 2024), Attorney General (2019, 2025), GST/101st Amendment (2017, 2023), Citizens’ Charter (2018, 2024), ONOE (2017, 2024), Constitutional morality (2021, 2025), RPwD Act (2017, 2022), Privacy/Art. 21 (2017, 2023, 2024, 2026), RPA corrupt practices (2020, 2025).
  • Recent trend (2024–26): more 15-mark questions with “compare with USA”, “cite case laws”, and judgements (privacy, euthanasia, collegium, Governor’s assent).
  • Questions are almost never pure facts – they ask Examine / Critically examine / Discuss. Laxmikanth gives the base (provisions, articles, cases); add analysis (issues, committees, way forward).
